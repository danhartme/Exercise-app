Unit 8

Present Perfect vs Past Simple

Use Past Simple for finished time periods (yesterday, in 2023, last sprint).

Use Present Perfect for unfinished time periods (today, this week) or when time is not mentioned.

"We launched the app in 2022." (Past Simple) "We have launched three features this week." (Present Perfect)
